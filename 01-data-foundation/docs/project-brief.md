# Shipping Data Foundation

*Linked research component · Prepared 5 November 2026*

## The research question

Can free data support an honest listed-shipping backtest?

## Approach

A dated 73-company register retains exits, identifies recycled tickers and rebuilds total returns from prices and distributions. FX and zero-volume treatment are explicit.

Shipping includes delistings, takeovers and distributions that can seriously distort a backtest. The register preserves those events and the gaps that remain.

## Evidence and result

The audit documents a bad FX print, a missed liquidating distribution and four recycled tickers that could manufacture apparent returns.

## Scope and limitations

Missing bankrupt-company histories mean survivorship bias is not eliminated. Source corrections need independent spot checks.
