# FleetWatch

*Linked research component · Prepared 5 November 2026*

## The research question

Can owner-attributed ship positions reveal information aggregate AIS misses?

## Approach

SEC-derived fleet records map roughly 1,000 ships to 21 owners; AIS identity and ship-state observations support a dated map and prospective company signals.

The research question depends on linking positions to specific listed owners, rather than assuming aggregate vessel traffic identifies a company-level edge.

## Evidence and result

A pre-registered US-waters idle-share test is null: the reported rank IC is -0.02 and t-statistic is -0.7 over 102 months. The first prospective scored season is Q4 2026.

## Scope and limitations

Identity matching, global reception and fleet coverage remain partial. First prospective scored season is Q4 2026; no live return edge is established.
